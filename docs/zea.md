# Meet ZEA

The official anime character and companion of ZKMNEXT GameSpace, in Void and Mini forms.

## GameSpace's own character

ZEA is the official anime character of ZKMNEXT GameSpace. She is the face and companion of your gaming space, with ZEA Void for the Character Room and ZEA Mini as your on-screen assistant.

## Void and Mini

Void Vanguard, Gothic Void, Crimson Reaper and Frost Void are ZEA's four outfits. Explore expressions, poses, actions and equipment in the Character Studio.

Void and Mini are two forms of the same character. Matching outfit and equipment IDs let compatible appearance choices follow both forms.

## Bring ZEA into the app

Open Virtual Character > Archive and import Zea-Void.zip and Zea-Void-Mini.zip. Select the full-size pack for the room and Mini for the assistant. Character artwork comes from imported packs.

MTP dialogs that use a character display the selected assistant. Character and dialog settings remain available in Character Studio and MTP studio.

The website home page features official ZEA artwork and actual Character Room, Dressing Room and Action Stage previews. All character previews use the latest ZEA Void packs.
