# Dashboard widgets

Choose the summaries and controls you actually need.

## Arrange your dashboard

Open Widgets and choose Customize. Select cards, use Up/Down to reorder them and choose Done. Choices and order are saved locally.

Cards can expand or collapse. FPS uses a primary number, CPU/GPU use paired meters and other controls can be condensed to keep the panel readable.

## Available cards

Frame rate, CPU/GPU, Performance, brightness, volume, tools, focus/alerts, monitor, crosshair, color, network, battery/thermal, quick settings and now playing.

Performance/Color/Charging shortcuts open their matching configuration category instead of duplicate editors. Read unavailable values as unavailable, rather than zero performance.
