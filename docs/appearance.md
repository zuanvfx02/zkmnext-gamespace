# Wallpapers, decorations and profiles

Personalize your space without changing system access.

## Wallpapers and atmosphere

Game space wallpaper includes backdrops, personal images, dimming, particles, vignette and Floating Wallpaper. Floating Wallpaper can be toggled independently, including with an AMOLED background.

Orbit preserves the original planets. Starflight uses rockets and satellites, Alien uses saucers and moons, and Crystal uses crystal shapes. Decorative objects do not capture touch input.

## Themes and profiles

Palette, AMOLED, scale and blur follow the relevant appearance settings. Compact continues to use performance-profile colors.

User profiles support an avatar, banner, decorations, name and statistics based on local usage. Per-game wallpapers can use available artwork.

## Animated wallpapers and rewards · v8.32

Handheld and Gamepad support photos, animated GIFs and muted looping videos up to 150 MiB. Select media in Game space wallpaper. Floating objects animate in both modes.

Common Android Motion Photos with embedded MP4 metadata are recognized. Other Live Photos require exporting and selecting the video. Codec support depends on your device.

Profiles include 20 achievements and free reward capsules. Each achievement grants one lifetime ticket and its direct decoration reward. Capsules grant a new permanent collection item with equal probability among remaining capsule items. Rune Circle has been removed.
