# Charging and bypass

Power controls require real device support.

## What bypass means

Devices expose different charging switches. Disabling battery charging does not necessarily create a direct hardware power path to the system.

Charging has its own menu. Read candidate status and scan results; Root alone does not prove support.

## Scanning and restoration

Connect a charger and follow the battery requirements. The scan tests candidate controls and checks their results. It includes thresholds for releasing bypass and cancel/restore paths.

If restoration fails, stop experimenting, restore the required access and use Restore before another scan. Check actual charger behavior on your device.

## Validation limits

Low current alone is not proof of bypass. Temperature, load, cable quality and the charging controller can all change current readings.

The charging workflow has not been validated on every model. This documentation describes source behavior, not hardware certification.
